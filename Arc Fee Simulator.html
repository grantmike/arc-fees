How the fee moves

every block (0.5 s):
  smoothed = 80% × previous smoothed + 20% × gas used in this block
  target   = 50% of the block gas limit
  next fee = fee × (1 + 2% × (smoothed − target) ÷ target)
  next fee is held between 20 and 20,000 gwei
  • Traffic stays at the level you set even as fees climb, so each curve is the fastest the fee can rise at that fullness. Real users would send less as fees rise.
  • Demand beyond a block's capacity waits for the next block. Spikes show up first as short queues, and only move the fee if they last long enough to lift the smoothed average.
  • A transfer is priced at 50,000 gas, which is what the docs' ~$0.001 floor figure implies. Fees are paid in USDC, and the base fee goes to the block producer instead of being burned.
  • Defaults are the mainnet fee settings read onchain on Nov 12, 2026, plus the 30M gas limit from the docs. Each run starts at the fee floor.
